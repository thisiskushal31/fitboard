### Added

- **Profile** — skills, experience, and preferences that drive scoring across the app.
- **Find jobs** — crawl public boards and career pages; rank postings by fit and region.
- **Applications** — track drafts through offer; generate tailored documents on your server.
- **Career path** — current, next, destination, and trending titles scored against your profile.
- **Startups** — YC and HN “Who is hiring?” companies ranked for outreach you send yourself.
- **Freelance** — contract and project gigs from HN, Freelancer.com, and remote boards.
- **Events** — conferences and meetups ranked by skill fit and place.
- **Agent** — AI help for search and drafting without Fitboard applying for you.
- **Inbox** — Gmail replies tied to applications when Google is connected.
- **Settings** — boards, AI providers, quiet days, and self-hosted password auth.
